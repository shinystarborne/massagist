Accessibility Notes

  • WCAG AA Standard: Requires contrast ratio of 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text
  • WCAG AAA Standard: Requires contrast ratio of 7:1 for normal text, 4.5:1 for large text
  • Cappuccino (#D2B48C): Good for backgrounds and subtle accents
  • Olive Green (#6B8E23): Excellent contrast on white, good for CTAs and links
  • Dark variants: Provide better contrast for accessibility compliance
